# Schedules: send statements, reports and payment plans automatically

Use schedules to run a task on a date or a repeating interval, so you don't have to send the same statement, report or invoice email by hand each time.

Manage schedules under **Settings > Schedules** (`#/settings/schedules`), in the Advanced Settings group (between Payment Links and User Management). The list has a **Filter** box, a **Lifecycle** filter, and **New Schedule**. Columns are **Name**, **Next Run**, **Frequency** and **Link**. Payment schedules for invoices also appear in this list.

You can also start some schedules from the record they apply to: **Schedule** in the menu next to **Save** on an invoice, **Actions > Schedule** on a report, and the **Payment Schedule** tab on an invoice.

**New Schedule** opens an unsaved form at `#/settings/schedules/create`. The **Template** options are **Email Statement**, **Email Record**, **Email Report**, **Invoice Outstanding Tasks** and **Payment Schedule**.

:::warning
A new **Email Report** schedule has **Send Email** turned on by default, even if Send Email was off on the Reports page. Review the form carefully, and do not click **Save** until you mean the emails to go out on the scheduled dates.
:::

Repeating schedules share this **Frequency** list: Daily, Weekly, Two weeks, Four weeks, Monthly, Two months, Three months, Four months, Six months, Annually, Two years and Three Years. There is no separate custom-interval option.

## How do I email client statements automatically? {#email-statement}

The **Email Statement** schedule sends client statements at the interval you choose, so clients get a regular picture of their account and outstanding balances.

1. Go to **Settings > Schedules** and click **New Schedule**.
2. Set **Template** to **Email Statement**.
3. Set **Next Run**, **Frequency** and **Remaining Cycles** (Endless, or a number of cycles).
4. Choose a **Date Range** (for example Last 7 Days, This Month or This Year) and a **Status** (All, Paid or Unpaid).
5. Optionally turn on **Show Aging Table**, **Show Payments Table**, **Show Credits Table** or **Only Clients with Invoices**.
6. Leave **Client** empty to include all clients, or pick a client.
7. Click **Save** when you are ready for the schedule to run.

Invoice Ninja generates the statement and emails it when the schedule runs. This form does not have a separate Send Email toggle.

## How do I email an invoice, quote, credit or purchase order on a set date? {#email-record}

The **Email Record** schedule sends an invoice, quote, credit or purchase order on a date you choose. Use this when you want precise control over when the client or vendor receives the document.

1. Go to **Settings > Schedules** and click **New Schedule**, or on an invoice open the menu next to **Save** and choose **Schedule**. That invoice shortcut opens the same Email Record form (`#/settings/schedules/create?template=email_record`).
2. Set **Template** to **Email Record** if it is not already selected.
3. Set **Next Run** to the send date.
4. Set **Type** to Invoice, Quote, Credit or Purchase Order, then select the document. If you started from an invoice, check that the invoice is selected; you may need to pick it yourself.
5. Optionally choose an email **Template**.
6. Click **Save** when you are ready for the email to go out on that date.

Email Record uses a single date (**Next Run**). It does not use Frequency or Remaining Cycles.

## How do I email a report automatically? {#email-report}

The **Email Report** schedule generates a report and emails it, so you or your team get regular figures without running the report by hand.

You can start this from **Settings > Schedules**, or from **Reports** with **Actions > Schedule**. That opens a **New Schedule** form (`#/settings/schedules/create?template=email_report`).

The form includes **Name**, **Template** (Email Report), **Next Run**, **Frequency**, **Remaining Cycles** (for example Endless), **Report**, **Range** and **Send Email**. **Send Email** is on by default, so check the report, range and other options before you save.

Aging reports, Profit and Loss, Expense and other report types can be scheduled this way. For the report types themselves, see [Reports](/docs/user-guide/reports).

## How do I invoice outstanding tasks automatically? {#invoice-outstanding-tasks}

The **Invoice Outstanding Tasks** schedule creates an invoice from outstanding tasks over a period you choose.

1. Go to **Settings > Schedules** and click **New Schedule**.
2. Set **Template** to **Invoice Outstanding Tasks**.
3. Set **Next Run**, **Frequency** and **Date Range**.
4. Optionally turn on **Auto Send** ("Automatically emails the invoice to the client") and **Include Project Tasks** ("Also invoice tasks that are part of a project").
5. Leave **Client** empty for all clients, or pick a client.
6. Click **Save** when you are ready for the schedule to create invoices.

This form does not use Remaining Cycles. For day-to-day task billing, see [Tasks](/docs/user-guide/tasks) and [Projects](/docs/user-guide/projects).

## How do I let a client pay an invoice in installments? {#payment-schedules}

A payment schedule splits an invoice into a series of payments. You can set one on the invoice, and the same schedules also appear under **Settings > Schedules**.

### From the invoice

1. Open a draft or sent invoice and go to the **Payment Schedule** tab.
2. Choose a schedule type:
   - **Split Payments** splits the invoice amount into multiple payments over a period of time, for example 4 payments over 4 months. Next you set **Number of payments**, **Frequency** and **First Payment Date**.
   - **Manually create a custom payment schedule** lets you set exact dates and amounts for each payment.
3. Complete the remaining steps, then save when you are ready.

You can set split amounts as currency amounts or as percentages. The client receives an email on each scheduled date for the amount due.

### From Settings > Schedules

1. Click **New Schedule** and set **Template** to **Payment Schedule**.
2. Select the invoice.
3. Optionally turn on **Auto Bill**. The help text is: "If enabled, when the schedule runs, auto bill will be attempted for the scheduled amount." This charges a saved payment method if a [payment gateway](/docs/user-guide/gateways) is connected and the client has a method on file.
4. Click **Save** when you are ready.
