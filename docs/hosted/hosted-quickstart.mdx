# Set up your hosted Invoice Ninja account

These short videos walk you through the first things to configure on a hosted Invoice Ninja account, from company details to sending your first invoice.

## How do I complete the initial setup? {#initial-setup}

This video covers the initial configuration of Invoice Ninja after you create a hosted account.

## How do I set my company defaults? {#company-defaults}

After that first setup, add the company details that appear on invoices, quotes, emails and the client portal. Go to **Settings > Company Details** (route `#/settings/company_details`). The tabs are **Details**, **Address**, **Logo**, **Defaults**, **Documents** and **Custom Fields**.

This video walks through some of those default settings:

<VideoPlayer src="/assets/videos/setup/setup.mpd" isDash={true} id="initial-setup-video" />

For the full field list, see [How do I set my company details?](/docs/user-guide/basic-settings#company_details).

## How do I set taxes and localization? {#taxes-and-localization}

<VideoPlayer src="/assets/videos/taxes/tax_configuration/tax_configuration_react.mpd" isDash={true} id="tax-configuration-video" />

Use this video to turn on invoice-level or line-item taxes, and to set language, currency and date formats for your region.

- Taxes: **Settings > Tax Settings** (route `#/settings/tax_settings`). See [How do I set tax defaults?](/docs/user-guide/basic-settings#tax_settings) and [Taxes](/docs/user-guide/taxes).
- Language, currency and dates: **Settings > Localization** (route `#/settings/localization`). See [How do I set language, currency and dates?](/docs/user-guide/basic-settings#localization).

## How do I connect Stripe? {#configure-stripe-payment-gateway}

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/qitfiD6L8rQ" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe>

This video shows how to connect an existing Stripe account, or create a new one, and link it to Invoice Ninja.

To do this in the app, go to **Settings > Payment Settings**. The page title is **Online Payments** (route `#/settings/online_payments`). That page lists any connected gateways and has an **Add Payment Gateway** button. For the full Stripe steps, see [Payment Gateways](/docs/user-guide/gateways) and [Hosted Stripe](/docs/hosted/hosted-stripe).

## How do I embed images on invoices? {#easily-embed-images-onto-invoices}

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/alGp17kfyjA" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe>
