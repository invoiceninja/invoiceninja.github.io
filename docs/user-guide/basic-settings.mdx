# Basic Settings: set your company, payments and defaults

Basic Settings is where you tell Invoice Ninja who your business is, how you work, and what new invoices, quotes and emails should start with. Most of what you set here flows into every document you send, so it is worth getting it right early.

If you are just getting started, the walkthrough in [Get Started](/docs/user-guide) covers the same ground in the order you will need it. When you are ready to go further into designs, reminders and custom fields, open [Advanced Settings](/docs/user-guide/advanced-settings).

:::info
The fastest way to find a setting is the search box in the top bar of the admin app (placeholder "Find invoices, clients, and more"). Searching for a term such as "tax" or "email" lists matching Settings pages from both Basic and Advanced Settings, along with other records.
:::

The left Settings menu is split into **Basic Settings** and **Advanced Settings**. Basic Settings, in order, is Company Details, User Details, Localization, Payment Settings, Tax Settings, Product Settings, Task Settings, Tags, Expense Settings, Workflow Settings, Account Management, Backup | Restore and Import | Export.

## How do I set my company details? {#company_details}

Everything in this section belongs to the company you currently have selected in the top-left company switcher. If you run more than one business from the same account, switch companies first. The settings on this screen apply only to the active company.

Go to **Settings > Company Details** (route `#/settings/company_details`). The tabs are **Details**, **Address**, **Logo**, **Defaults**, **Documents** and **Custom Fields**.

The name, logo, address and contact details you enter here appear on your invoices, quotes, emails and the client portal. The terms and footers you set under Defaults are what every new invoice, quote, credit or purchase order starts with.

### Details

The first block of fields prints onto most default document layouts, so treat them as your letterhead. Emails and websites become clickable links in PDFs.

| Field | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Company Name** | Your company name. |
| **ID Number** | A business or tax identification number. |
| **VAT Number** | An EU or UK style value added tax identifier. |
| **Website** | Your company's website. |
| **Email** | A general or sales contact address. |
| **Company Phone** | A phone number clients can reach you on. |
| **Company Size** | Approximate headcount, chosen from a dropdown. |
| **Industry** | A searchable list of preset industry categories. |
| **Classification** | An optional classification, such as Business. |

### Address

These standard address fields print on default invoice, quote and statement layouts: **Street**, **Apt/Suite**, **City**, **State/Province**, **Postal Code** and **Country**.

### How do I add a company logo? {#logo}

Set your company logo under **Settings > Company Details > Logo**. The heading is **Upload Your Company Logo**. Use **Drop files or click to upload**, then **Crop Logo** or **Remove logo** if you need to adjust or clear it.

Company branding is used on customer-facing documents and the client portal. To change portal colors or style the logo area, see [Client Portal customization](/docs/user-guide/client-portal#customization). A roughly 300 x 300 pixel image works best. Much larger files can cause layout issues when generating PDFs. A landscape logo usually reads best across emails and the portal.

### What defaults apply to new documents? {#defaults}

The **Defaults** tab holds the standard terms and footer text for each document type. They fill in automatically when you create a new record, and you can still change them on an individual document.

| Field | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Invoice Terms** and **Invoice Footer** | Default wording on new invoices. |
| **Quote Terms** and **Quote Footer** | Default wording on new quotes. |
| **Credit Terms** and **Credit Footer** | Default wording on new credits. |
| **Purchase Order Terms** and **Purchase Order Footer** | Default wording on new purchase orders. |

Use these for standing disclaimers, late-fee clauses or policy wording you want on every document. They print below any public notes.

Payment due dates and quote validity are not on this tab. Set **Payment Terms** and **Quote Valid Until** under [Payment Settings](#payment_settings).

### Documents

The **Documents** tab is a shared file store for company documents. It has a **Set Documents Public by Default** toggle ("If enabled, new documents will be public by default.") and a **Drop files or click to upload** zone. Uploaded files appear in a table with Name, Date, Type and Size.

### Custom Fields

The **Custom Fields** tab on Company Details is for extra fields on the company record. To add or rename fields for clients, invoices and other records, see [Custom Fields](/docs/user-guide/advanced-settings#custom_fields).

## How do I update my user details? {#user_details}

This section is about you, the currently signed-in user, rather than the company. Your user details identify you to teammates, receive password-reset emails, and tie you to any client, task or project you have been assigned.

Go to **Settings > User Details** (route `#/settings/user_details`). The tabs are **Details**, **Password**, **OAuth / Mail**, **Two-Factor Authentication**, **Accent Color**, **Notifications**, **Custom Fields**, **Preferences** and **Keyboard Shortcuts**.

| Field | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **First Name**, **Last Name** | Your name, as shown to teammates. |
| **Email** | Used for password resets and team contact. |
| **Language** | Your preferred language in the admin app. |
| **Phone** | For reference. |
| **Email Signature** | A personal signature you can add to messages. |

### Password

The **Password** tab has a **New password** field. Your current password is not shown, and there is no current-password field on this tab.

### Two-Factor Authentication

The **Two-Factor Authentication** tab has a **2FA** row and a **Passkey** row, each with an **Enable** button. Open this tab when you want to turn either method on. Do not enable it unless you intend to set it up on this account.

### Accent Color

The **Accent Color** tab has a color picker. Accent color is the highlight and button color used throughout the admin portal. It is per user and per company, so you can tell companies apart at a glance.

### Notifications

The **Notifications** tab controls which events email you personally.

At the top are these toggles:

| Setting | Help text |
|---|---|
| **Login Notification** | "Sends an email notifying that a login has taken place." |
| **Task Assigned Notification** | "Send an email when a task is assigned" |
| **Disable Recurring Payment Notification** | "Successful recurring invoice payment notifications will not be sent." |
| **Enable E-Invoice Received Notification** | "Receive an email notification when a new E-Invoice is received." |

**All Events** can be set to **All records**, **Owned by user** or **Custom**. Each event then has its own dropdown: **All records**, **Owned by user** or **None**.

The event list includes Invoice Created, Invoice Sent, Invoice Viewed, Invoice Late, Inventory Threshold, Payment Success, Payment Failure, Payment Manual, Purchase Order Created, Purchase Order Sent, Purchase Order Viewed, Purchase Order Accepted, Quote Created, Quote Sent, Quote Viewed, Quote Approved, Quote Expired, Quote Rejected, Credit Created, Credit Sent and Credit Viewed.

## How do I set language, currency and dates? {#localization}

Localization controls how currency, dates, numbers and language appear on your documents. Set this early. Changing currency after you have issued invoices does not recalculate the ones you have already sent.

Go to **Settings > Localization** (route `#/settings/localization`). The tabs are **Settings** and **Custom Labels**.

### Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Currency** | Your company's default currency. Individual clients can override this. |
| **Currency Format** | How amounts are presented: **Symbol** (for example `$ 1,000.00`) or **Code** (for example `1,000.00 USD`). |
| **Language** | The default language for documents and emails. |
| **Time Zone** | The company's time zone. |
| **Date Format** | How dates are presented. |
| **24 Hour Time** | Toggle 24-hour time. |
| **Rappen Rounding** | Optional rounding used in some currencies. |
| **Decimal Comma** | "Use comma as decimal place in forms." |
| **First Month of the Year** | Anchors annual reports to your fiscal year. |

### Custom Labels

Custom Labels let you rename a built-in term across your documents. That is useful when your industry uses a different word, or when you want to change one phrase without switching the entire app language.

The Custom Labels tab has a dropdown of available labels and an **Add Custom** button. Choose a label from the dropdown (the list is the label text, such as Amount, Due Date or VAT Number), then add it and type the replacement you want shown.

Edit or create a new invoice to see your relabelling applied.

## How do I set tax defaults? {#tax_settings}

Tax Settings is where you decide whether tax lives at the invoice level, the line-item level, or both, and how many tax rates each document can carry. Set this up before your first invoice. Changing it later does not rewrite invoices you have already sent.

Go to **Settings > Tax Settings** (route `#/settings/tax_settings`).

:::warning
It is easy to tax twice by enabling tax on both invoice totals and every line item. Use line-level tax when different products carry different rates, and invoice-level tax when one rate applies to the whole document. For deeper tax scenarios, such as multiple jurisdictions, compliance modes and per-client tax rules, see [Taxes](/docs/user-guide/taxes).
:::

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Invoice Tax Rates** | How many tax rates can be applied to the invoice total: **Disabled**, **One Tax Rate**, **Two Tax Rates** or **Three Tax Rates**. |
| **Line Item Tax Rates** | The same choices, for tax rates on each line item. |
| **Expense Tax Rates** | The same choices, for expense records. |
| **Inclusive Taxes** | When the toggle is off, the example shown is "Exclusive: 100 + 10% = 100 + 10". |
| **Calculate Taxes** | "Automatically calculate taxes when saving invoices." |
| **Default Tax Rate** | A dropdown on a separate card, used as the default rate when invoice tax rates are enabled. |

The tax rate list sits on the same page, below those options. It has **Filter**, **Lifecycle** (Active) and **New Tax Rate**. The columns are Name, Tax Rate and Actions.

**New Tax Rate** opens an unsaved **Add Tax Rate** form with **Name** and **Tax Rate**. Give the rate a name and a percentage, then save it, and it becomes selectable from the tax dropdowns on invoices, products and expenses.

## How do tags work? {#tag_settings}

Tags are named, colored labels you attach to records so you can filter, search and report on related work without inventing a new custom field for every label. Typical uses are marking a client as a retainer client, flagging tasks as billable, or grouping invoices that belong to a campaign.

You manage the catalog from **Settings > Tags** (route `#/settings/tags`). The page title is **Tags**. You apply tags on the record itself. Clients, vendors, products, projects, tasks, invoices, quotes, credits, recurring invoices, expenses, recurring expenses, payments, purchase orders and transactions all have a Tags field.

On any of those screens, start typing in the Tags box to pick an existing tag or (if you are an admin) create a new one on the spot. List views can filter by tag, search includes tag names, and [Reports](/docs/user-guide/reports) can show a Tags column and restrict the export to selected tags.

The Tags page has **Filter**, **Lifecycle**, a **Type** filter, and **New Tag**. The list columns are Name, Type, Color and Actions.

Keep the catalog small and reuse names. A handful of well-chosen tags is much more useful than a unique label on every record.

### What is Global Tag Inheritance?

**Global Tag Inheritance** is a toggle on the Tags page, off by default. The help text is "Determines whether child entities inherit tags from their parent entities."

Turn it on when a label on a parent should automatically land on the records you create underneath it. For example, tagging a client so every new invoice, quote, project and task for that client starts with that tag already applied.

Only **global** tags cascade. A task-only or project-only tag stays on that record and is never copied to children.

When inheritance is on, a brand-new record picks up the union of global tags from the parents it already has:

| Parent | New records that inherit its global tags |
|---|---|
| **Client** | Projects, tasks, invoices, quotes, credits, recurring invoices, expenses, recurring expenses, payments and purchase orders. |
| **Vendor** | Expenses, recurring expenses, purchase orders, invoices, quotes and credits (when a vendor is set), and transactions. |
| **Project** | Tasks, invoices, quotes, credits, expenses, recurring expenses and purchase orders tied to that project. |

Tags you set on the new record yourself are kept and merged with whatever was inherited. Products never inherit. They have no parent.

Two things inheritance does not do:

- It runs **once, at creation**. Assigning a project or client later does not backfill tags.
- Changing a parent's tags later does not update children that already exist.

With inheritance on, invoicing also rolls global tags up from the tasks and expenses you add as line items. That roll-up is additive and runs whenever you save the invoice, so tagging a billed task as a global tag will add that tag to the invoice without removing tags already there. Entity-specific task tags are left behind.

A workflow that works well:

1. Create the labels you want to travel as **global** tags (**Settings > Tags > New Tag**, type **Global**).
2. Tag the parent, usually the [client](/docs/user-guide/clients) or [project](/docs/user-guide/projects).
3. Create the child records. They arrive already tagged.
4. Use typed tags on the child for local workflow that should not spread.

Leave inheritance off if you prefer to tag each record by hand, or if you often create a task first and attach the project later.

### How do I create a tag?

There are two ways to add a tag to the catalog.

From **Settings > Tags**, click **New Tag**. That opens an unsaved form with:

- **Name**: the label you will see on pills and in filters. Commas are not allowed (they conflict with how filters store multiple values). Names must be unique for that type in the company, including archived tags. If a name already exists as a task tag, restore it instead of creating a second one.
- **Type**: **Global**, or one record kind. The type list includes Global, Transaction, Client, Credit, Expense, Invoice, Payment, Product, Project, Purchase Order, Quote, Recurring Expense, Recurring Invoice, Task and Vendor. Type is locked after you save.
- **Color**: optional. Used on the pill so related tags are easy to scan.

From a record, type a new name into the Tags field and choose **Create**. That creates a tag of that record's type (a task form creates a task tag, not a global one) and attaches it immediately. Only admins can create tags this way. Everyone else can still attach tags that already exist.

If you want a tag to inherit, create it as **Global** in Settings first, then attach it to the parent. Creating a tag from an invoice form makes an invoice-only tag that will never cascade.

### How do I edit, archive or delete a tag?

Open a tag from the list in **Settings > Tags** to rename it or change its color. The type cannot be changed. Make a new tag of the other type if you picked the wrong one.

On an active tag, the row **Actions** menu has **Edit**, **Archive** and **Delete**.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Archive** | Hides the tag from pickers and filters. Existing assignments stay on records, and the name remains reserved, so you can restore it later. |
| **Delete** | Removes it from pickers. You cannot attach a deleted tag to new records. |

Archiving is the safer cleanup when a label is out of season but you still want history and reports to make sense.

### What tag types can I use?

Every tag belongs to exactly one type. A picker only offers **global** tags plus tags of that record's type, so a task never shows invoice-only tags.

| Type | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Global** | The label should follow a client, vendor or project onto new work, or appear on more than one kind of record. Required if you want inheritance. |
| **Client** / **Vendor** | Qualifiers that only matter on the company record. |
| **Project** / **Task** | Delivery workflow. Task tags do not copy onto invoices unless they are global and inheritance is on. |
| **Invoice** / **Quote** / **Credit** / **Recurring Invoice** / **Payment** | Billing and collections. |
| **Expense** / **Recurring Expense** / **Purchase Order** / **Transaction** | Cost-side grouping that is not already covered by [expense categories](/docs/user-guide/expenses). |
| **Product** | Catalog flags. Products do not inherit from anything. |

The same name can exist once per type. An Urgent task tag and an Urgent project tag are two different tags and do not substitute for each other. If you find yourself creating the same name on every type, make one **global** tag instead.

## How do expense settings work? {#expense_settings}

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|

| **Mark Paid** | "Track the expense has been paid." This records that you paid the vendor, not that a client paid you. Payment Type, Date and Transaction Reference are not on this settings page (they appear on the expense itself). |
| **Convert currency** | "Set an exchange rate when creating an expense." |
| **Add Documents to Invoice** | "Make the documents visible to client." |
| **Notify Vendor When Paid** | "Send an email to the vendor when the expense is marked as paid." |

:::

An expense category list sits at the bottom of the same page, with **Filter**, **Lifecycle**, **New Expense Category**, and columns for Name, Color and Actions.

## How do I back up or restore a company? {#backup_restore}

**Settings > Backup | Restore** (route `#/settings/backup_restore`) is how you download a full company backup or load one back in. The tabs are **Backup** and **Restore**.

### Backup

The Backup tab has the label **Create company backup**, the help text "Once the file is ready you'll receive an email with a download link", and an **Export** button. Click Export only when you want that email sent to you.

### Restore

The Restore tab has the label **Select company backup file**, the help text "Please upload the .zip file used to create this backup.", a **Drop files or click to upload** zone, **Import Settings** and **Import Data** options, and an **Import** button.

A restore overwrites the currently selected company, so only import into the company you mean to replace. If you want the backup as a new, separate company, create the empty company first and switch into it before importing.

## How do I import or export my data? {#import_export}

**Settings > Import | Export** (route `#/settings/import_export`) is how you bring a spreadsheet or a file from another invoicing tool into Invoice Ninja, or export a CSV of a record type. For the full import and export steps, including how to map CSV columns to Invoice Ninja fields, see [Import and Export](/docs/advanced-topics/import-and-export).

The page has two sections, with no tabs.

### Import

**Import Type** chooses the source format. The list includes FreshBooks, Invoice2go, Invoicely, Quickbooks, Wave Accounting and Zoho. Depending on the type, you then get upload zones (for example **Clients** and **Invoices**, each with **Drop files or click to upload**) and an **Import** button.

### Export

Export on this page is **CSV** only. Choose an **Export Type**, then click **Export**. The types include Activities, Clients, Client Contacts, Invoices, Invoice Items, Quotes, Quote Items, Credits, Documents, Expenses, Purchase Orders, Purchase Order Items, Recurring Invoices, Payments, Products, Vendors and Tasks.

A full company backup (settings and data as a downloadable zip, emailed when ready) is not on this page. Use [Backup | Restore](#backup_restore) for that.

## Device Settings {#device_settings}

Device Settings is no longer a page in the Settings menu, and `#/settings/device_settings` is not a valid route. Some appearance controls, such as a dark-mode icon, sit in the admin sidebar instead.
